# Langwa: demo video script (under 3 minutes)

For the team recording the SAFINUM Innovate Challenge demo. Seven scenes, each tied to a scoring
criterion. About 390 spoken words, which fits in 2:50 at a calm pace. Speak French or English;
add subtitles in the other.

## Before recording

- Fresh browser profile in **Edge**, Langwa installed from the website (that shows the real install).
- **Offline natural voice running and warmed** (`server\start-voice.cmd`, wait for "✓ Natural voice").
  Do not use the online voice in the video: rule 9 forbids transmitting financial data.
- Record the screen at 1080p with system sound (OBS: Display Capture + Desktop Audio). Browser zoom 110 %.
- Close other tabs and notifications. Open `safinum.cm/hackathon-test` on the balance page.
- Know in advance how to make a new message appear on the test page (for example an amount above
  the balance, or the test protocol). Rehearse scene 5 twice.
- Record each scene separately, then join them. Keep cuts quick; no music under the voice.

## Scenes

| # | Time | Criterion (points) | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00 – 0:15 | Problem | The SAFINUM Pay balance page in French. Slow zoom on "Frais" and "Total à débiter". | "Many Cameroonians read Pidgin, Fulfulde or Ewondo more easily than French. In a banking app, one misread word, a fee or a beneficiary, can cost money. This is Langwa." |
| 2 | 0:15 – 0:35 | Integration and independence (20) | Toggle Langwa off and on in the popup: the bank page is identical. First visit: the language chooser, search "pidgin", pick it. The guided tour starts; show the voice step for one second, then Skip. | "Langwa is a browser extension. It reads the bank page and never changes it: no code, no form, no request is touched. Turn it off and the page is exactly the same. Pick your language once." |
| 3 | 0:35 – 1:20 | Functionality: the journey (25) | **Balance:** Ctrl + point at "Solde disponible", the card opens, press **Hear am** (natural voice). **Costs:** go to step 2, point at "Frais", then open the panel and ask **"Wetin dis page be?"**; the answer plays. **Beneficiary:** point at "Bénéficiaire" → "Person wey go receive am". **Summary:** point at "Total à débiter". | "Point at any text and the translation appears right under it, written by a native speaker. Press Listen and a natural voice reads it. The assistant explains each step of the transfer: balance, charges, the person who receives, and the summary to check before confirming." |
| 4 | 1:20 – 1:50 | Security of financial information (20) | Zoom on the locked chips (25 000 FCFA, Jean-Paul Mbarga). Settings page → **Banc d'essai**: type "Vous allez envoyer 25 000 FCFA à Jean-Paul Mbarga." and run it. Show the masked text (⟦A⟧, ⟦B⟧), the protected values list and "Contrôle d'intégrité : OK". | "Amounts, fees, names and references are locked before any translation and checked after. If a translation changed a single digit, it would be blocked and the exact original shown. Everything runs on this computer: no account, nothing sent anywhere." |
| 5 | 1:50 – 2:20 | Adaptation to new content (10) | Trigger a new message on the test page (error or security warning). Langwa's alert appears, marked NEW, with the meaning in Pidgin and the exact amounts, read aloud. | "When a new message appears, even one Langwa has never seen, it is detected at once. Warnings come first, the amounts stay exact, and if there is no validated translation, Langwa says clearly what kind of message it is instead of guessing." |
| 6 | 2:20 – 2:45 | Accessibility and user experience (15) | Translate-page button (every text in Pidgin), then Read all. Quick cut: the phone version (long press). Quick cut: the contribution page, a speaker translating a sentence and **Send (1)** on WhatsApp. | "Translate the whole page, or let Langwa read it aloud, on a computer or a phone. Native speakers add and check translations from their phone, so every language keeps improving." |
| 7 | 2:45 – 2:58 | Feasibility and documentation (10) | The website: "Nine languages" with coverage bars, then the install section. End card: Langwa logo and the site address. | "Pidgin and Fulfulde today, seven more languages in progress, all validated by native speakers. Free, documented, and ready to install. Langwa: online banking, in your language." |

## Checks before submitting the video

- Under 3:00, sound clear, no personal data, no real account.
- Every Pidgin line on screen reviewed by a native speaker (major defects are scored hard).
- Scene 5 shows a message that was not prepared in the glossary.
